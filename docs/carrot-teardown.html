CARROT is not a 2026 design reference

The bundle carries 160 .nib files, 2 storyboards, and 11 SpriteKit scenes on a minOS 15.0 baseline — a UIKit/Interface-Builder app on a 2021 floor. SwiftUI appears three times in the main binary. Its modern code is quarantined in one place: the widget extension, which links SwiftUI + WidgetKit + ActivityKit and declares NSSupportsLiveActivities.

What carries this app is personality and breadth, not visual craft. Mine it for feature surface. Look elsewhere for a 2026 visual language.

Their "Feels Like" is one opaque number

Across 3,201 strings there is exactly one relevant key — data_point_type_feels_like → "Feels Like". No breakdown, no per-premium decomposition, no ledger, nowhere. The category leader ships the very thing your PRD calls opaque and generalized.

Your transparent math is the moat. Everything below is surface area you could add; none of it is a reason to trade that away.

Gap analysis

Scored against RealTemp as it stands after today's v2 pass. Ordered by fit with your thesis — the temperature your body actually meets on the street, with the math visible — not by how impressive the feature is.

FeatureStatusWhat CARROT ships
Personal weather stations Missing Netatmo + WeatherFlow sign-in, a station map, station picker, per-station detail. The single most on-thesis feature they have — your whole premise is that Stevenson-screen readings lie about the street, and a nearby PWS is literal street-level truth. Would also give you real data to tune coefficients against, which N2 was gated on.
Wet-bulb temperature Missing Shipped as a first-class data point and card. This is the heat-stress metric — the one that defines survivability limits. You already have air temp and dew point, which is everything the calculation needs.
Custom notification rules Partial A rule engine: any data point, above/below/rises/drops, threshold or scheduled, over a time window, with daytime/overnight scoping and Do Not Disturb hours. Named presets for Sunscreen and Umbrella reminders. You have one hardcoded daily heat check.
Solar / daylight detail Partial Golden hour, blue hour, civil / nautical / astronomical dawn and dusk, first and last light, daylight averages. You compute solar zenith already — this is presentation on top of math you have, and it sits directly on your sun-premium story.
Forecast confidence (ensemble) Missing Ensemble model spread rendered as HIGH/LOW confidence: "if the lines are tightly grouped the forecast is likely to hold; if widely spread, conditions are uncertain." Honest uncertainty is very much your brand — you already show your working.
Composable layouts Missing 4 app styles (Card / Full Screen / Multi-Column / Plain), 4 card styles, 3 configurable rows, ~25 placeable data points, saved layouts, and different layouts per weather condition (clear night, fog, rain, snow). This is their "build your own weather app" pitch and the core of the paywall.
Widgets + Live Activities Missing Home Screen, Lock Screen, Notification Center, Smart Stacks. Live Activities auto-start on launch showing a short-term precipitation chart. Needs a native Swift extension inside your Capacitor shell — real work, and blocked behind the Xcode setup verify:ios-prereqs still fails on.
Map layers Partial 12 presets: precipitation, super-res radar, temperature, wind, humidity, dew point, cloud cover, visibility, air quality, rain totals, snow depth, wildfires. You ship radar + shade, and shade is something they don't have at all.
Almanac / history Missing Historical records, all-time stats, a sunny/cloudy/rainy/snowy day grid, and personal "high scores" for hottest day and highest UV. Their own chart admits it needs 7 days of use before it populates.
Air quality + pollen Missing AQI as data point, card, map layer and notification; pollen count as a card. Both affect whether you want to be outside — arguably adjacent to your actual question.
Severe weather alerting Partial Dedicated extensions for hurricane tracking (all 5 categories + tropical depression/storm/cyclone), lightning-strike proximity with compass direction, and storm-cell tracking. You proxy official AEMET/MeteoAlarm CAP alerts, which covers the Spain case they'd handle generically.
Trip planning Missing Multi-stop trips with per-stop forecasts and a days-until countdown.
Apple Watch Missing Full companion app, a watch-face gallery, custom complication slots, configurable Infograph bezel text, and ~15 complication types.
Siri + App Intents Missing Current-forecast intent with custom UI, an interaction intent, and nlu.appintents shipped per language.
Tides / moon phases Skip Tide stations with rising/falling charts; moon phase with its own notification schedule. Coastal Valencia makes tides faintly plausible; neither touches perceived temperature.
SPC outlooks Skip US Storm Prediction Center convective and fire-weather outlooks, days 1–8. US-only — irrelevant in Valencia.
Zodiac Skip All 12 signs with date ranges. Yes, really.
Personality / voice Skip Selectable assistant personality, a profanity toggle, speech synthesis, secret locations, achievements, and original songs shipped as MP3s. This is their entire brand and the reason people pay. Competing here means becoming a different product.
