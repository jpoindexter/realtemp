RealTemp · design direction · 2026-07-06

Street instrument, not weather postcard.

Every weather app sells sky photography. RealTemp sells a calculation you can trust — so the UI borrows from receipts, field instruments and ledgers: tabular numerals, signed deltas, hard rules, one accent spent on heat. High-contrast light ground because it's read in direct Valencia sun.

  • The receipt is the hero’s proof. Number rules the screen; the ledger under it explains it. Pattern lifted from Acorns’ value + breakdown and YNAB’s signed category rows.
  • Everything on one screen, no scroll — glance-on-the-street use. Density benchmark: CARROT’s observation grid, without its 16-metric sprawl.
  • Toggles are the product’s verbs — full-width segmented controls, thumb-reach bottom half, 44px targets. Selected = solid ink, not tint.
  • Color is semantic only: warm = adds heat, blue = removes it. Direction encoded twice (sign + color) for color-blind safety.

04 · Anti-patterns (the moat)

  • Sky/cloud photography backgrounds (Apple Weather owns it; kills contrast in sun)
  • Weather-condition icon zoo — conditions are numbers here, not cartoons
  • Opaque “Feels like 38°” with no receipt — the entire reason this app exists
  • Gradient hero cards, glassmorphism — fights legibility at 100k lux
  • Scroll-to-see-more on the core screen
